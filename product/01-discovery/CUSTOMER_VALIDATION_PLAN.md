# Independent customer validation plan

Independent validation is outstanding. Proposed participants: 3–5 Chief Underwriters, 3–5 Underwriting Managers, 2–3 Analytics Specialists, 2–3 Customer Success Managers and 2–3 Product or Commercial Leaders.

Research should ask about actual past optimisation behaviour, frequency and severity of investigation pain, data availability, policy configuration, trust in recommendations, preferred scheduled or on-demand workflows, adoption barriers and commercial demand. Avoid leading questions and do not present this plan as completed research.
