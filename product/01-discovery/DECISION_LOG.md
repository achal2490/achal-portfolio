# Phase 1 decision log

- **PD-001:** Complement the underwriting engine rather than replace it.
- **PD-002:** Complement existing analytics dashboards rather than build another conventional BI product.
- **PD-003:** Focus on proactive rule discovery even when STP is stable.
- **PD-004:** Design for users without advanced analytical expertise.
- **PD-005:** Evaluate B2B2B as the leading commercial hypothesis.
- **PD-006:** Treat underwriting philosophy alignment as a gating constraint.
- **PD-007:** Preserve Chief Underwriter decision authority.
- **PD-008:** Investigate historical simulation as a potential capability.
- **PD-009:** Preserve existing testing, phased rollout and monitoring.
- **PD-010:** Evaluate agentic AI against simpler alternatives.
- **PD-011:** Base MVP data requirements on realistically available application, rule and outcome data.
- **PD-012:** Keep scheduled and on-demand investigation as proposed requirements.
- **PD-014:** Do not infer underwriting rationale solely from final outcomes.
- **PD-017:** Use synthetic data and protect confidential information.
