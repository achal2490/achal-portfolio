# Phase 1 discovery report

## Working problem statement

Life insurers seek to increase straight-through processing (STP) to reduce underwriting cost, accelerate application processing and allow specialist underwriters to focus on complex cases. Mature platforms capture application, rule-execution and final-outcome data, but many insurers lack the dedicated analytical resources needed to discover opportunities to expand automated coverage. Optimisation therefore depends on infrequent, specialist-led investigations.

## Core insight

The opportunity is not another dashboard. It is scalable, proactive discovery of actionable rule-coverage opportunities using existing data and established approval, testing, rollout and monitoring processes.

## Discovery findings

- Manual investigation of large referral populations is effort-intensive and specialist-dependent.
- Final underwriting decisions and ratings were available in the described environment; detailed manual reasoning and evidence-collection history were not.
- Underwriting philosophy is insurer-specific and may not be structured or machine-readable.
- The Chief Underwriter remains the final approval authority.
- Scheduled and on-demand investigation are product preferences requiring independent validation.
- A B2B2B model is a commercial hypothesis, not a validated decision.

## Confidentiality

The public case study uses an anonymised recurring-activity example and does not publish insurer-specific values, rules, applicants or proprietary materials.
