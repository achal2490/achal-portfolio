# Underwriting IQ master plan

## Objective

Explore a product that proactively discovers recurring manual referral patterns and prepares evidence-backed underwriting rule improvement proposals for expert review. It complements existing underwriting engines, dashboards and human decision authority.

## Lifecycle

1. Product Discovery — internal discovery complete; independent validation outstanding.
2. Opportunity Assessment and Business Case — next phase.
3. Product Strategy — not started.
4. Requirements and Experience Design — not started.
5. Agentic AI Solution Design — not started.
6. AI Experimentation and Evaluation — not started.
7. Product Development and Delivery — not started.
8. Responsible AI and Production Readiness — not started.
9. Commercialisation and Go-to-Market — not started.
10. Launch and Continuous Improvement — not started.

## Evidence boundary

Phase 1 is based on three retrospective sessions with one domain expert, grounded in firsthand professional experience. It is not three independent customer interviews, market validation or product-market fit evidence.

## Next gate

Proceed to Opportunity Assessment and Business Case without approving full-scale engineering investment.
