# Development journal

## Phase 1 — Internal discovery complete

**Investigated:** domain workflow, referral optimisation, stakeholders, jobs to be done, data feasibility, governance constraints and commercial ecosystem.

**What changed:** the initial concept assumed detailed manual reasoning and evidence history would be available. Discovery established that the realistic described dataset contains final decisions and ratings, so the product must discover associations and prepare proposals without claiming to know why an underwriter decided.

**Key learning:** requirements and architecture must reflect actual source data availability. Underwriting philosophy is a decision constraint, not a prompt for the system to invent policy. Multi-agent orchestration is an option to evaluate, not a product requirement.

**Next:** Phase 2 opportunity assessment, market and competitor research, AI suitability, build-versus-buy, ROI, unit economics and commercial business case.
